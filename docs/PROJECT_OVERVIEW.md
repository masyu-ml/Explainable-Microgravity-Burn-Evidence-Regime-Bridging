# EMBER Project Overview

EMBER (Explainable Microgravity Burn Evidence & Regime Bridging) is One Man Orbit's NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 research prototype for the Flame in Freefall challenge.

The evidence engine retrieves source-linked NASA BASS-II experiments with weighted Gower-style nearest-neighbor ranking. The physics engine calculates bounded material/geometry-specific quantities and may perform constrained inverse regime comparisons. A separate oxygen-only Bayesian ignition reference is protocol-specific and must not be presented as a universal Earth, Moon or Mars prediction.

**Scientific guardrails:** An experimental source observation is distinct from an inferred or computed value. Similarity is not ignition probability or equivalence of combustion behavior. Unsupported physical comparisons should be marked with scientific abstention rather than invented predictions.

**Sources:** [NASA PSI-25](https://psi.nasa.gov/physci/repo/data/investigations/PSI-25), [NASA BASS-II Technical Report](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20210011385).
