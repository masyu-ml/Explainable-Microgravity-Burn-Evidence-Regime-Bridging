## Scientific labeling contract

Each result should identify:

1. Original NASA record and source location.
2. Fuel, geometry and recorded environmental conditions.
3. Whether each field is measured, report-derived, model-derived, missing or disputed.
4. Applicable retrieval, statistical or physics model.
5. Source-specific limitations and abstention when unsupported.

Experimental conditions, numerical similarity scores and physics estimates are not interchangeable with validated flame outcomes. Archived evidence CSVs and scientific report tables require independent source and checksum review for any safety-relevant use.
