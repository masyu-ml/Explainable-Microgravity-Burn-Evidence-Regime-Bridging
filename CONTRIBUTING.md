# Contributing to EMBER

Contributions should preserve traceability, scientific reproducibility and accuracy.

- Explain the NASA source record or scientific publication underlying any new numerical claim.
- Provide equations, dimensional units, validity ranges, domain restrictions and regression checks for model modifications.
- Preserve clear separation of experimental observations, model-derived physics values, and protocol-specific statistical estimates.
- Do not add unsupported claims of cross-gravity equivalence or certification.
- Avoid committing access tokens, private data, credentials or deployment configuration.
- Confirm licensing terms with the maintainer before redistributing third-party source and datasets.

Open an issue describing the proposed change, its evidence basis, and expected results.
